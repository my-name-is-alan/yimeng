# 译梦 — Personal Music Library (self-hosted)

## Product context
Non-commercial personal music app. Core on NAS; Win/Mac desktop clients (also design mobile-friendly views inspired by reference). Metadata from MusicBrainz + Apple Music + 网易云 + QQ Music (user VIP). Screens: Album + Artist (+ home/player vibe from style ref).

## Visual direction (LOCKED to user reference)
Dark contemporary music UI — NOT Roon-dense coral editorial.

- Background: deep charcoal / near-black `#0A0A0C` – `#121216`
- Surfaces: soft elevated cards `#1A1A1F` with large radius `20–28px`
- Accent PRIMARY: lime / chartreuse `#C8F542` (active pills, play button, progress, focus)
- Secondary accents: soft purple `#7B5CFF` / magenta `#E85DFF` for featured cards & gradients only
- Text: white `#FFFFFF` primary, `#A1A1AA` secondary
- Glass: frosted bottom dock / bars — `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`, semi-transparent dark
- Imagery: circular album/artist art preferred in player; rounded-square in lists
- Aura: blurred color wash behind Now Playing pulled from cover colors
- Typography: clean geometric sans (Inter / SF Pro style), friendly weights, generous spacing
- Layout: more spacious than Roon; friendly consumer feel, still desktop-capable

## Shell
- Optional left nav OR bottom glass dock (Home / Radio / Library / Search / Settings)
- Category pills with lime active state
- Featured purple gradient cards for discovery
- Bottom player or full Now Playing with circular art + lime controls

## Album / Artist pages
Keep information (tracks, credits, sources, editions) but restyle to this language: lime accents, larger radius, softer cards, glass bars, purple featured moments. Chinese UI chrome.

## Do not
- Coral `#FF6B50` accent (superseded)
- Ultra-dense Roon spreadsheet feel
- Harsh white marketing / serif editorial
